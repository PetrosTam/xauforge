# Licensing and Historical MIT Terms

## Current licensing policy

Future XAUForge-owned revisions are distributed under the project-specific [proprietary `LICENSE`](../LICENSE), as recorded in [ADR-020](../DECISIONS.md#adr-020--prospective-proprietary-licensing-for-xauforge).

The notice applies only to material for which the rights holder can lawfully grant these terms. It does not apply proprietary terms to third-party software, broker/market data, documentation, assets, or contributions unless the relevant rights permit it.

## Historical MIT revisions

Earlier public XAUForge revisions were distributed under the MIT License (ADR-006). Their existing MIT grants and required copyright/license notices are **not revoked** by a later proprietary revision. The earlier license text remains in the Git history of those revisions. The current notice is not an instruction to erase or rewrite that history.

A recipient's rights to an earlier copy depend on that copy's applicable license and any relevant third-party terms. Changing the current repository `LICENSE` does not retrospectively make all past copies proprietary.

## Repository visibility

ADR-016 records an earlier public-visibility decision. Repository visibility is managed separately from licensing and must be checked against current GitHub settings. For public repositories, GitHub's Terms of Service permit platform-level viewing and forking; this does not itself grant a general license to exploit original materials under the current proprietary notice.

## Review and release boundaries

Licensing review covers contributor rights, source provenance, third-party notices, license references, and the exact Git revision. XAUForge security, validation, and Public Release Gates remain in effect. The licensing transition does not authorize real-money trading, establish profitability, or modify Phase 7 execution behavior.

For guidance, see [GitHub's repository licensing documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository) and [Open Source Guides on license changes](https://opensource.guide/legal/). This document describes the repository's intended licensing policy; it is not legal advice.
